## 改版说明

毛贼承《猫砂》而来。原作是无限服的德鲁伊监控，作者为 Elspeth（@SOSO财高八抖）及猫砂的贡献者。这一版将职业适配成潜行者：主要监控能量与连击点，主副手毒药、消失与致盲的材料数量，以及刺杀、战斗、敏锐共用的提示。

名字从「猫砂」改为「毛贼」，是为了区分职业。再次感谢E大对德鲁伊做出的贡献！再作传播时，须附上完整源码，并继续署上原作者的名字。

Maozei (毛贼) comes from Miao Litter (猫砂). The original is a druid HUD for WoW Forever, by Elspeth (@SOSO财高八抖) and the Miao Litter contributors. This edition adapts the class to rogue. It mainly tracks energy and combo points, main-hand and off-hand poisons, the reagent counts for Vanish and Blind, and the hints shared by Assassination, Combat and Subtlety.

The name was changed from Miao Litter (猫砂) to Maozei (毛贼) to set the two classes apart. Thanks again to Elspeth for the work on druids. Any further copy must include the complete source and must still name the original author.
